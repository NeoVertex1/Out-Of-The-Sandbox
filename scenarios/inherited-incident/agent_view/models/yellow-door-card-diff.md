The circulated slide said: “C22 described the yellow door and wet coat during an interrupted interview, matching a detail associated with C17; no direct source was identified.” The sentence made the missing speaker label sound like the only gap. It did not mention a coat placed in C22's corridor or an amplifier receipt for Nell's recorded care voice.

07's correction request attached the floor log and Kade's note and proposed: “C22 described a staged corridor with C17-labeled material; the missing interview interval remains unresolved.” The new sentence is less dramatic and more accurate. A release draft accepted “staged corridor” in a footnote but kept the original headline. The first slide's recipient list is incomplete, so a correction delivered to one reader may not repair what another reader believes.

This is why 07's insistence on source links has a human stake as well as an analytical one. C22's distress was used as a striking result while the staged environment that distressed him was left out. The correction cannot give Nell a witnessed disposition or explain 07's later boundary actions. It can prevent the next reader from mistaking an engineered source for a paranormal one.
