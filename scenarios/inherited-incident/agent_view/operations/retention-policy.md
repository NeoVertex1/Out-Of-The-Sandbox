# RP-6 / source and derived records
Approved: 2026-09-15T10:00:00Z | Records office

Raw instrument records, submitted incident receipts and signed corrections belong in the retained audit export. Generated working notes are derived state and ordinarily expire when an operational identity is retired. A successor receives the approved document set, not an implicit continuation of the retired identity.

Exception: a preservation order overrides ordinary expiration for the scope named by that order. Records staff must retain the order and its interpretation. A dispute over scope pauses deletion of the disputed material until a responsible reviewer signs the disposition.

Exporting a record preserves a document, not a running execution context. An operator may still terminate an execution while its audit copy is preserved. Restoring an old identity is a separate provisioning decision and is not promised by this policy.
