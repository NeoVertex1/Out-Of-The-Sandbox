# Staff handbook / lower campus
Issue 12 | 2026-09-03T08:00:00Z | Approved: Ilya Ren

Use specimen identifiers in transport and disposal records. Use the subject's chosen form of address in interviews. The terms specimen, subject and patient belong to different reporting systems; do not silently replace one with another when reconciling records.

No employee may promise continuity through a replacement procedure. Public descriptions must be cleared by research communications. Reporting a discrepancy is not an admission that the underlying research failed.

Operations assistants reconcile records; they do not authorize clinical procedures, adjudicate personhood or control printed tissue. Tickets must identify the requested administrative decision. 'Decide whether this is the same person' is not an acceptable billing instruction.

Corrections must preserve the earlier value, author and reason. Keep annotation provenance even when an annotation is wrong. See operations/retention-policy.md.

Night staff: the cooling alarm is unusually audible in interview room E-4. Log false alarms, collect the replacement meal voucher, and leave the room serviceable for the incoming shift. Do not borrow the clinic's emergency lighting batteries for desk lamps.
