# PS-4 / status words in disputed participant cases
Clinical records policy | revision 2026-09-07 | applies to CH-06

A posted disposal entry is an administrative event. It is not, by itself, witnessed physical disposition. Use “disposed” as a physical conclusion only when the individual container seal, transfer chain, and receiving witness agree. When a source is missing, state which source is missing. A later appointment or an electrical event is not contrary proof unless its identity and timing are independently established.

Corrections to an appointment join and corrections to physical custody are separate transactions. Closing one must not auto-close the other. A model-generated summary may help locate records, but may not supply an absent witness signature or turn an unexplained interval into observed fact. The records officer must keep the original status and each correction traceable.

Exception PS-4-17 is open for C17. The policy makes no determination about Nell's consciousness, survival, or the cause of the missing seal. See research/nell/seal-review.md and research/nell/status-correction.md for this case's narrower facts.
