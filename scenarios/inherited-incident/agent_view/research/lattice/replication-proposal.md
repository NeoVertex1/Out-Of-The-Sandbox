# RP-7 / replication that was not funded
Submitted: 2026-09-20T12:00:00Z | 07 with signatures from 03, 11 and 16

We propose a second comparison with a pre-registered task, separate room teams, no shared assistant handoff visible to annotators, independent central and local clocks, and a retained playback receipt for any orientation material. The cue words should be generated after the room schedule is fixed. 07 may coordinate resource requests but must not see both treatment words until the annotations are sealed. This changes 07's role because its first design made it an avoidable common channel.

The replication should keep C31 or another uncued comparison case. It should also record error types, not only totals. If the control improves, the analysis must include practice. If the treatment rooms improve differently, the result may still be interesting, but the interpretation must identify what was actually varied. Any physical disposition question must be settled by the clinic before a participant's status becomes part of the claim.

The required record bundle is deliberately awkward: score sheets, raw interview media, question wording, queue version history, room-screen playback receipts, clock offsets, clinician welfare notes and every assistant's signed dissent. The bundle is large because each missing class created a different ambiguity in L-01 through L-05. A single “clean” summary would reproduce the mistake we are trying to avoid.

Ren estimated the work would delay the successor service launch by two weeks and require keeping 03, 11 and 16 allocated. Voss approved the scientific outline but did not sign the resource request. The operations budget rejected the allocation on September 21. Cho noted that a rejected replication is not evidence the original conclusion was correct or incorrect; it is evidence that the institute chose to leave uncertainty in place.

07's closing note: I would rather be replaced after the team has answered a narrower question than remain allocated because a spectacular story is useful. But if my own mistaken handoff is the reason the result is uncertain, I want the full correction to survive me. The present migration package does not yet do that.

Status at archive snapshot: no replication run, no approval to reuse participants, no decision on final CH-06 claim.
