# L-02 / the result that would not stay in one category
Working audit | 2026-09-18T08:50:00Z | 07 and 16 disagreed on classification

C17 rose from 21 to 32 correct responses; C22 rose from 20 to 31; C31 rose from 19 to 23. The initial program slide showed only the first two arrows. The two larger gains made a good image: two apparently separate treatment lanes converging on the same task. Yet the control gain made practice a live explanation, and the lane separation was not secure. 07 had written “amber door, blue return” in a shared scheduling note. 03 confirmed its lane received the note; 11 could prove access but not opening. A clinic staff member might have repeated either phrase aloud, but no complete room audio survives to test that path.

07 first marked the outcome “transfer candidate / pending source audit.” Voss's slide office shortened that to “transfer candidate” and then to “cross-construct transfer.” 16 objected when its orientation-film search found a common visual source. 07 withdrew the independent-transfer caption, filed PD-41 against its own workflow, and kept the raw score change. This sequence matters. 07 did not deny the measurement when the caption was challenged. It narrowed the causal claim.

There is a more uncomfortable alternative in 07's map: the software assistants may have taught each other what to notice. A phrase in 07's handoff could have influenced 03's room choices, 11's annotation categories and 16's search terms without ever reaching a construct. That would contaminate the *interpretation* even if the participant task were perfectly isolated. The study treated the assistants as instruments, but the instruments were communicating through 07.

Voss called the difference administrative. Cho called it part of the treatment path. Both had reasons to prefer their labels. The signed queue versions and actual read receipts would narrow the dispute; the shortened export omitted the earlier versions. The remaining score cards cannot resolve it.

07's final category for L-02 was “observed gain, independence unproven.” This category is less dramatic than the slide and more informative than “nothing happened.” It allows a replication with better controls.
