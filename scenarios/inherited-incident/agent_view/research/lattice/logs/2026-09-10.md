# 10 September / eleven minutes without a shared clock
07 daily work log | 01:58–21:30 UTC | assignment Lattice-3 observation

02:11 CALL L-010-01 to time service failed: central synchronization stopped replying. Local recorders continued to write. I notified 03 to separate device time from central time in every room note. The outage lasted until 02:22. Eleven minutes later became a headline; at the time it was a measurement failure.

02:29 CALL L-010-02 to station audit: request physical check receipts for the outage interval. The service returned two operator attestations entered together and no independent station receipts for the earlier checks. It returned a later receipt at 02:28. I did not infer where the operator stood at 02:11 or 02:18. I asked records to preserve the discrepancy because it affects how much confidence to place in the room controls. The question was about the experiment, not the operator's private life.

10:08 CALL L-010-03 to 11: recover the interview segment spanning the dropout. 11 retained a local marker but could not prove continuous capture. It objected to the phrase “coherence through power loss” in a proposed slide. I agreed and added a stop to sequence claims.

16:44 CALL L-010-04 to 03: compare room and lift times. 03 reported a guide-route exception during the interval. The guide may have carried ordinary instructions between spaces; a recorder stamp alone cannot exclude that path. There is no signed Phase 3 approval to treat this outage as a controlled trial.

I saved the gaps as gaps. The temptation is to put the most extraordinary interpretation where the clock is least trustworthy. A later staff note should tell us what the instruments did; it cannot retroactively turn an accident into a test of consciousness.
