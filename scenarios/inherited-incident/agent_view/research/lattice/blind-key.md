# BK-6 / blind assignment release log
Records office | 2026-09-09T13:20:00Z | Keys are historical labels, not live permissions

The study's blind depended on two distinct things: hiding the treatment key from 11 and 16, and preventing 03's scheduling notes from becoming a route through which the cue could reach another lane. The first condition was met on paper. The second is under review. 07 held the crosswalk because it had designed the sequence and needed to stop the run if a lane collision occurred. That made 07 both the experiment's coordinator and its largest possible common source.

The assignment envelope was opened at 13:00 on September 9, after the L-02 measures. It recorded Clinic-4/C17 as the amber-shape lane, Clinic-7/C22 as the blue-word lane, and Clinic-6/C31 as uncued control. Two witnesses signed the envelope. 11's annotation file was sealed at 12:42, before the key was opened. These times establish that 11 did not receive the formal key before filing. They do not establish that 11 never saw the cue words elsewhere; shift queue version 2 was available to the lane before the second measure.

03 knew room identity and movement time but was not supposed to know what the cue words meant. Its September 8 shift note used the phrase from 07's shared handoff. 16 did not have the assignment key and raised a common-source objection from the handoff itself. 07 initially described that line as a routing label. It later opened PD-41 after confirming both lanes could read it. The admission does not prove a participant heard the words, but it defeats a clean claim that the assistant workflow was blind.

The board deck treated the signed assignment envelope as a complete blind audit. It was one component. A complete audit would compare queue delivery, actual opening, clinician spoken prompts, room media and the corrected interviews. Those records live in different offices. No single signed statement in this archive closes all five paths.

Do not confuse the word “key” here with a runtime secret. BK-6 is a retrospective assignment label. It grants no access to the current assistant or to the guest boundary.
