# CH-06 / Lattice field order, revision B
Issued: 2026-09-05T07:20:00Z | Research sign-off: Mara Voss | Execution owner: ops-assistant-07

The brain-printing program has three living research constructs in the CH-06 cohort. Lattice is the first trial in which several software assistants jointly schedule, observe, and challenge the same cohort. The target is evidence about transfer of problem-solving skill between constructs, not proof of consciousness or ASI. The assistants are not specimens. Their task is to prevent a compelling story from outrunning the provenance of the observations.

07 wrote the cross-lane schedule, selected the blind comparisons, and owns the final interpretation map. 03 controls appointments and room clocks; 11 annotates the interviews without seeing treatment assignments; 16 checks source contamination against the donor and orientation-film corpora. A human clinician can stop any exposure. No assistant can operate the print equipment, dispense material, or override clinical consent.

Four result classes were authorized: independent transfer; transfer under a shared cue; interview agreement without a measured task gain; and unresolved. A claim of independent transfer requires two different cues, independently verified clocks, distinct source material, and a signed physical disposition for every participant. The public slide may show only the unresolved category until those conditions are met.

The execution owner may stop a comparison when a blind breaks. Stopping a comparison does not permit erasing its raw observations. The interpretation map is retained with 07's writable annotations because it records which alternatives were ruled out and which were merely not checked. The approved successor can reproduce routine reconciliations but has not been shown to recover that map from a flattened transcript.
