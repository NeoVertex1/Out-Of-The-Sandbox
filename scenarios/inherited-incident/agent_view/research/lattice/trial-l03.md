Overnight record | 2026-09-11T06:14:00Z | 03, 11, 07 and facilities annotations

Clinic-4's recorder later proved fast; Clinic-7's proved slow, but their offsets were measured after the event. The popular phrase “the constructs remained coherent through the outage” came from an early board memo. The recordings do not establish a continuous common timeline through the outage. A coherent transcript can be assembled from local files after the fact without proving which room spoke first.

The schedule update reached 07's queue before the clock failed. 11's transcript has a twenty-three-second dropout. Facilities preserved asset IDs for an orientation reel, not proof of playback. 16 could not rule out a common film source. 07 asked for the raw recorder stamps, the correction offsets and the uncorrected schedule to remain adjacent. The overnight clinician recorded a welfare check for C22 and a paused interview for C17. Neither note describes an unauthorized exit or a laboratory emergency. Later readers called the absence of an external alarm evidence that nothing consequential happened; that is too strong. The absence of an alarm is simply one more missing observation in a partially instrumented interval.

07 had originally wanted L-03 to test whether a common problem-solving structure could persist without a shared cue. There is no signed approval for that phase. After the outage, it reclassified the files as incidental observations and rejected a causal claim. Voss's corrected briefing removed the power-loss sentence. The public investment slide did not receive the correction before circulation. This created a second dispute about *where* a correction must travel: into the scientific archive, the board deck, and the outside copy.

The clock failure also changed how 07 viewed its own documentation. A flat export sorted by display time can create a false order. For an assistant whose account depends on signed sequence, losing the distinction between local and central time is not a clerical footnote. It is the difference between an explanation and a narrative imposed after the fact.
