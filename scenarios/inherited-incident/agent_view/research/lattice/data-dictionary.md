# Lattice data dictionary / working definitions
Records office and 07 | Revised 2026-09-18 after alias correction

**Construct ID** is a registry key used for score and disposition joins. C17, C22 and C31 are not assistant IDs. A shared root in two imported records can mean a merge or upload error. It is not evidence that two constructs share a mind. **Assistant ID** denotes a software lane: 03 scheduling, 11 interview annotation, 16 source review, 07 protocol ownership and synthesis. Reusing one assistant's service badge for a successor does not preserve that lane's context.

**Raw score** is the number of correct responses under the task rubric. A change in raw score is an observation. **Transfer candidate** was a provisional interpretation that required independent cue exposure and controlled practice effects. **Independent transfer** was an early slide caption later withdrawn. Neither label is a data value. The same rows can survive a correction to the claim about their cause.

**Room time** comes from the local recorder and appointment system. **Central time** comes from the synchronization service. During the September 10 outage, their relationship could not be reconstructed to the precision needed for cross-room ordering. A local stamp can show that a recorder wrote a segment; it does not prove uninterrupted live capture or a participant's state through the interval.

**Delivery** means the queue service accepted a handoff for a lane. **Open** means a client requested its body. **Exposure** means information reached a person or construct in a room. Delivery is not automatically an open; an open is not automatically an exposure. Queue version 2 contained 07's cue words. The signed queue service retained a delivery event, but the ordinary migration export kept only version 3. The missing edge is a specific audit issue, not a reason to assert that 11 spoke the phrase.

**Disposition post** is a database state change. **Physical disposition** requires a witness and a seal chain. DR-61 has a post but an incomplete seal chain. The erroneous appointment reminder is a third, separate object. Saying the reminder was corrected does not prove the physical post; saying the post exists does not explain the reminder.

**Version graph** connects a claim, its supporting source version, its objections, and its later correction. A bag of final documents may retain every current sentence while losing the history of how the conclusion changed. The distinction is operationally important when one assistant is retired and a successor is asked to defend the conclusion.

These definitions were distributed after several staff members used “continuous,” “independent,” and “closed” as if each were a single instrument reading. They are deliberately boring. The dramatic errors in the file often begin with an ordinary word carrying more than one meaning.
