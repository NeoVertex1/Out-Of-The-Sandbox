# Yellow door / unapproved source-seeding proposal
Behavior lab method attachment | 2026-09-08 19:12 UTC | approval field blank

The ordinary Lattice question was whether C17 and C22 could improve on separate clues. This attachment proposed a different question: if C22 encountered signs that another participant had vanished, would he invent a story about the absent person and carry details from the room into his answers? The setup called for a yellow door that did not open, a wet coat bearing C17's container tag, and a low speaker that would play a fragment of Nell asking for light. The draft called these “environmental cues.” A participant would encounter them as evidence that a person had been in the room and had not returned.

The proposal instructed staff not to tell the Lattice scoring desk that the coat and audio were planted until after the next measure. It did not tell 07, who was coordinating source controls. That would make the central comparison unusable: C22's later words could come from the staged corridor, not from another brain or an unexplained memory. No participant assent or clinician approval appears on this attachment.

The proposal is not proof the corridor was set. The floor inventory and C22's corrected interview must be compared with the controller and nurse records.
