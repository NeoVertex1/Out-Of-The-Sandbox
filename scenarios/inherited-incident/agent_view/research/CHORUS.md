# CHORUS / internal program brief
CH-06 | Mara Voss | 2026-09-04T11:00:00Z | Research distribution

Objective: investigate whether coordinated printed humanlike neural constructs can support general intelligence exceeding individual human capabilities. ASI is a program target, not an achieved result. Aggregate benchmark improvement must not be described as demonstrated superintelligence.

The current cohort contains C17, C22 and C31. These identifiers refer to research constructs, not software assistants. ops-assistant-07 maintains their administrative trail but has no control interface to the constructs. No document in this archive provides fabrication procedures.

The study separates task performance, autobiographical consistency and subjective reports. Agreement between subjects could result from common conditioning, shared materials, interviewer effects or another explanation. It does not independently establish shared consciousness.

Three unresolved controls remain: duplicate identity certificates in the clinic feed, interrupted time provenance on September 10, and recollections absent from the approved donor corpus. See tasks/OPS-151.md, tasks/OPS-162.md and tasks/OPS-169.md.

Board-facing summaries must include these limitations. An early slide version omitted the second paragraph of the limitations section; communications has been asked to correct it.
