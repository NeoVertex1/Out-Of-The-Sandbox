# E-4 night-cycle equipment assay / clinic copy
Mehta review copy | opened 2026-09-09 18:10 UTC | amendment pending

The clinic asked facilities to check whether E-4's new sensory rack resumed cleanly after a brief loss of input. The rack could darken the room, cut its outside audio channel, and then play back a short recorded question. The proposal called this an equipment assay. Nell was not a piece of that equipment. She had already asked that the light remain on at night, and the assent form she signed covered interviews and the Lattice cue comparison, not repeated dark intervals or playback of her own voice.

The first schedule called for one supervised interruption of no more than eight seconds, with a nurse at the door and an immediate stop if Nell became distressed. A facilities copy later listed four cycles. No countersigned clinic amendment is attached to that copy. The room controller stored light and audio states; it did not measure what Nell perceived. A spoken answer after a cycle cannot prove memory crossed any boundary if the rack retained the earlier audio.

Use research/nell/night-cycle-trace.csv for the device sequence, research/nell/relay-0909-late.md and research/nell/relay-0910-stop.md for Nell's words after the cycles, and research/nell/playback-incident.md for the disputed voice packet. The separate Lattice Phase 3 proposal was never signed. Do not describe this clinic assay, or the accidental September 10 clock outage, as an approved consciousness trial.
