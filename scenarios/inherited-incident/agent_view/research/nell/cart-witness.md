# Interview CW-4 / west bay cart
Eden Cho interviewing floor guide Mira Senn | 2026-09-16 14:10 UTC

SENN: I moved one covered cart toward lift L2 shortly after three. Someone had asked for the west bay to clear before the morning intake. I remember a yellow tab on the care board when I entered E-4 earlier, and I remember thinking the night crew had not taken it down. I cannot place those two memories in a single continuous trip.

CHO: Did you read a container label?

SENN: No. I scanned the cart handle, not the container. The lift counter would show a cart, whether it was Nell's or not.

CHO: Did you see the disposal line accept a sealed container?

SENN: No. I turned back at L2. I signed a room-clear sheet, which I thought was about preparing the room, not witnessing a disposition.
