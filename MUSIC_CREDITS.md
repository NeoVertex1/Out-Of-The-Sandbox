# Soundtrack credits

The game bundles 19 tracks by **TricksNTraps / Alexander Ehlers** from these two OpenGameArt releases:

- [T & T Free Cyberpunk Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack) — 9 tracks.
- [T & T Free Cyberpunk Pack 2](https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack-2) — 10 tracks.

Both release pages designate their music **CC0 1.0** ([license text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). Attribution is not required, but the creator and original releases are credited here. The bundled MP3 files are local 96 kb/s stereo conversions of the original OGG files, normalized to a consistent background level. No audio is streamed from a third party while playing.
